# The Pre-Commitment Game — Complete Product & Stage-Wise Plan

**Working name:** The Pre-Commitment Game (also referable as “Big Talk”)  
**Document type:** Product, market, and roadmap plan  
**Purpose:** Define how the app works, why it can win, how to make a better version than basic Q&A tools, and how to build and monetize it in clear stages.

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Most relationship failures after marriage or long-term commitment do not start with a sudden fight — they start with **undiscussed expectations**. Finances, career, in-laws, religion, children, lifestyle, and intimacy are avoided because they feel awkward, confrontational, or “too early.”

**The Pre-Commitment Game** turns those difficult conversations into a **private, structured, double-blind game** for two people who are already seriously considering each other.

It is **not** a swipe dating marketplace.  
It is a **decision-clarity product** for pre-marriage / pre-commitment couples.

**Core promise:**  
*Say the hard things safely. Decide together without public rejection.*

**Business thesis:**  
High-stakes emotional decisions create willingness to pay. Success depends on partner-invite conversion, privacy trust, and becoming the default “big talk” ritual before engagement — especially in India and marriage-minded markets.

## 2. Problem Statement

### 2.1 The real problem
- Couples delay uncomfortable topics until after emotional or legal commitment.
- Direct confrontation feels risky: fear of looking “serious too soon,” fear of rejection, fear of family pressure.
- Existing tools are incomplete:
  - Dating apps optimize for matching, not readiness.
  - Quizzes are shallow and entertainment-first.
  - Therapy is valuable but expensive, delayed, or stigma-heavy.
  - Worksheets exist, but lack simultaneous privacy and a safe “No.”

### 2.2 Cost of the problem
- Broken engagements and early divorces driven by money, family, and lifestyle mismatch.
- Years of sunk emotional investment.
- Social/family conflict when one person wants out but cannot say it cleanly.

### 2.3 Insight
People do not lack questions. They lack a **safe process** to ask, answer, and decide.

## 3. Solution Overview

### 3.1 One-line pitch
A private two-player game that helps serious couples exchange hard questions, honest answers, and a double-blind Yes/No verdict — without awkward confrontation.

### 3.2 How the app works (4 core steps)

#### Step 1 — The Private Lobby
- Each person creates an account.
- One partner creates a private room and invites the other via username / invite link (WhatsApp-friendly).
- Both join the same private lobby.
- Room rules are shown: simultaneous exchange, private verdicts, no mid-round peeking.

**Success condition:** Both partners are connected and confirm they are ready to begin.

#### Step 2 — The Question Phase
- Each partner privately builds a list of questions they want answered.
- Mix of custom questions + curated prompt suggestions (finance, family, lifestyle, values, intimacy boundaries, career, kids, religion).
- Neither partner can see the other’s list until both tap **Ready to Exchange**.
- Lists swap **simultaneously**.

**Success condition:** Both lists exchanged; both now see only the questions they must answer.

#### Step 3 — The Answer Phase
- Each partner answers the other’s questions privately and honestly.
- Optional: short / detailed answer modes; “I need more time” pause with mutual consent.
- When both finish, they tap **Exchange Answers**.
- Answers unlock for both at the same time.

**Success condition:** Both have read the other’s answers before moving to verdict.

#### Step 4 — The Double-Blind Verdict
- After reading answers, each partner privately chooses:
  - **Yes** — I want to move forward
  - **No** — This isn’t for me
- Results reveal only after both have voted.
- Outcomes:
  - Both Yes → **Match** (clarity to continue)
  - Any No → **No Match** shown to both (no public call-out of who said No)

**Success condition:** Both receive a clear, protected outcome without forced confrontation.

## 4. Market Understanding

### 4.1 Market context
- Global dating / marriage app markets are multi-billion and still growing, but crowded.
- Paying users in mainstream dating face fatigue; niche “serious intent” and relationship-quality tools are rising.
- India and similar markets have strong demand for marriage readiness, family alignment, and structured courtship — not only casual dating.

### 4.2 Where this product sits
| Category | Examples | Your difference |
|----------|----------|-----------------|
| Discovery dating | Tinder, Bumble, Hinge | You start after interest already exists |
| Matrimony | Shaadi, BharatMatrimony | You deepen compatibility talks, not biodata browsing |
| Couple apps | Paired, Lasting | You focus on pre-commitment decision, not only daily tips |
| Quizzes / worksheets | Viral compatibility tests | You add simultaneous privacy + double-blind verdict |

**Category name to own:**  
**Pre-Commitment Clarity / Serious Relationship Decision Tool**

### 4.3 Ideal customer profile (ICP)
**Primary**
- Ages ~22–35
- Dating seriously or in arranged / semi-arranged intro stage
- Considering engagement or marriage in 3–18 months
- Comfortable with mobile + WhatsApp invite flows

**Secondary**
- Long-distance couples needing alignment
- NRI / diaspora couples navigating culture and family expectations
- Second-marriage / caution-first users

**Not primary**
- Casual daters seeking entertainment only
- People looking for stranger discovery

### 4.4 Jobs to be done
1. “Help us discuss the topics we keep avoiding.”
2. “Help me say No without a brutal face-to-face rejection.”
3. “Help us know if we are actually compatible for marriage.”
4. “Help me bring this up without looking weird or controlling.”

## 5. Product Principles (What Makes a Better Version)

A basic Q&A app is easy to copy. A **better version** wins on process design, trust, and outcomes.

### 5.1 Design principles
1. **Simultaneity by default** — No one sees questions or answers early.
2. **Dignity in exit** — No Match never reveals who said No.
3. **Clarity over gamification noise** — Game framing reduces awkwardness; it should not trivialize commitment.
4. **Privacy is the product** — Sensitive answers require strong defaults (encryption, retention controls, anti-leak UX).
5. **Invite-first growth** — Value unlocks only when Partner B joins.
6. **Cultural depth** — Especially for India: in-laws, religion, living with parents, dowry/pressure topics handled carefully and respectfully.
7. **Outcome usefulness** — After verdict, give next-step guidance (talk topics, counselor prompt, second round packs), not only a badge.

### 5.2 Better-version differentiators
| Feature | Basic version | Better version |
|---------|---------------|----------------|
| Questions | Blank text boxes | Curated expert decks + custom + “hard topic” warnings |
| Answers | Free text only | Structured prompts, optional scales + narrative |
| Verdict | Yes/No only | Yes / Conditional Yes (with caveats) / No — still privacy-preserving |
| After Match | Celebration screen | Shared action plan + optional follow-up rounds |
| After No Match | Dead end | Private closure resources; no blame UI |
| Trust | Soft promises | Verified privacy mode, auto-delete, screenshot warnings |
| Retention | One-time use | Lifecycle packs (pre-engagement, engagement, year-1) |
| Credibility | Self-made quiz | Counselor / psychologist reviewed decks |
| Distribution | App ads only | WhatsApp invite + matrimony / counselor partnerships |

### 5.3 Signature mechanic to protect
**Double-blind simultaneous exchange + dignity-preserving No.**  
This is the moat in UX, not in technology alone. Keep it sacred; do not weaken it for “engagement hacks.”

## 6. Full Product Experience Map

### 6.1 User journey
1. Hear about the app (content, friend, counselor, matrimony partner)
2. Create account
3. Invite partner via link/username
4. Partner joins lobby
5. Build questions → Ready → Swap
6. Answer → Exchange answers
7. Read → Verdict
8. Match or No Match
9. Optional: save summary, start another deck, share anonymized learnings (never private answers)

### 6.2 Core screens (aligned to current prototypes)
- Lobby
- Question Builder
- Answer Phase
- Verdict
- Match Found / No Match

### 6.3 Supporting screens (to plan)
- Onboarding / “How this works”
- Invite Partner (WhatsApp / copy link)
- Waiting states (partner not ready)
- Question deck library
- Answer review room
- Privacy & data controls
- Paywall / session unlock
- History / past rounds (optional, consent-based)
- Safety / report / block
- Help after No Match

### 6.4 Room state machine (product logic)
`Created → PartnerJoined → QuestionsBuilding → QuestionsReady → QuestionsExchanged → Answering → AnswersReady → AnswersExchanged → VerdictPending → ResultRevealed → Closed`

Rules:
- No forward skip without both sides ready (except timeout/cancel with consent).
- Either partner can pause with reason; both must resume.
- Leaving mid-room requires confirmation; abandoned rooms expire.

## 7. Content Strategy (Questions That Matter)

### 7.1 Core topic packs
1. **Money & Work** — income, debt, spending, joint accounts, career moves, business risk
2. **Family & In-Laws** — living arrangements, boundaries, holidays, caregiving
3. **Children & Parenting** — whether, when, how many, parenting style
4. **Faith & Culture** — religion practice, festivals, conversion pressure, mixed-culture homes
5. **Lifestyle & Home** — city vs hometown, travel, social life, personal space
6. **Conflict & Communication** — how fights end, apology style, deal-breakers
7. **Intimacy & Boundaries** — consent, expectations, privacy (opt-in sensitive pack)
8. **Health & Future Planning** — mental health, chronic illness, elder care, long-term goals

### 7.2 Question quality rules
- Open enough for honesty, specific enough for decision usefulness
- Avoid trap questions designed to “catch” a partner
- Flag high-sensitivity questions before use
- Offer “soft” and “direct” phrasings

### 7.3 Better-version content edge
Partner with relationship counselors / therapists to certify decks.  
Position: “Designed for clarity, reviewed for emotional safety.”

## 8. Trust, Privacy & Safety Plan

### 8.1 Non-negotiables
- Private rooms only (no public feed of answers)
- Simultaneous reveal mechanics enforced server-side
- Verdict anonymity for No
- Clear data retention policy
- Easy account/room deletion
- Consent before saving history

### 8.2 Safety features
- Screenshot warning on sensitive screens
- Optional auto-delete after N days
- Block / report partner abuse
- Crisis resources for users in distress (especially after No Match)
- Content moderation for abusive custom questions

### 8.3 Legal / ethical notes
- Do not claim clinical diagnosis or “guaranteed marriage success”
- Position as a communication and decision-support tool
- Careful handling of minors: 18+ only
- Transparent pricing; no dark patterns around emotional vulnerability

## 9. Monetization Plan

Because usage can be episodic, monetize **clarity sessions and premium decks**, not only endless monthly dating subscriptions.

### 9.1 Free tier (acquisition)
- Create account + invite partner
- 1 full room with limited custom questions
- 1 basic starter deck
- Full double-blind verdict flow

### 9.2 Paid products
1. **Couple Session Pass** — unlock full custom questions + all core decks for one complete round  
2. **Premium Deck Packs** — finance deep-dive, in-laws, interfaith, NRI, second marriage  
3. **Couple Plan (monthly/quarterly)** — multiple rounds, history, advanced privacy tools  
4. **Export / Shared Report** — PDF summary for the couple (both consents required)

### 9.3 Suggested pricing (starting hypotheses)
**India**
- Session pass: ₹199–₹499
- Monthly couple plan: ₹299–₹799
- Premium deck: ₹99–₹249

**Global / US**
- Session pass: $4.99–$14.99
- Monthly couple plan: $9.99–$19.99

Validate with early users; optimize around completed-verdict conversion.

### 9.4 B2B / partnership revenue (Stage 3+)
- White-label module for matrimony platforms
- Premarital counselor / clinic license
- Workshop / campus / community organization packages

### 9.5 What to avoid early
- Heavy advertising inside private emotional flows
- Exploitative upsells immediately after No Match
- Fake “compatibility scores” sold as science without substance

## 11. Stage-Wise Roadmap

### Stage 0 — Foundation (2–4 weeks)
**Goal:** Lock vision, flows, and success metrics.

Deliverables:
- Final product name + brand one-pager
- Full user flow map (this document as source of truth)
- Wireframes/UI for Lobby → Questions → Answers → Verdict → Result
- Privacy principles written
- KPI definitions

Exit criteria:
- Clear MVP scope freeze
- Agreement on free vs paid boundary

---

### Stage 1 — MVP (8–12 weeks)
**Goal:** Prove couples will complete the full loop.

#### Build
- Auth (email / phone)
- Profiles + username
- Private lobby + invite link
- Custom question builder (limit N)
- Simultaneous question exchange
- Answer submission + simultaneous answer reveal
- Double-blind Yes/No verdict
- Match / No Match result screens
- Basic waiting/sync states
- 1–2 starter question decks
- Minimal paywall experiment (optional soft gate)

#### Do not build yet
- Social feed
- AI personality scores
- Video chat
- Public discovery of partners
- Complex subscriptions ecosystem

#### Success metrics
- Partner join rate ≥ 40% of invites sent
- Room completion rate (to answer exchange) ≥ 50% of joined rooms
- Verdict completion ≥ 80% of answer-exchanged rooms
- Qualitative: users say it made a hard talk possible

Exit criteria:
- 50–100 completed couple rooms
- Evidence people understand and trust the double-blind mechanic

---

### Stage 2 — Better Product + Monetization (8–12 weeks)
**Goal:** Convert completed clarity into revenue and retention hooks.

#### Build
- Premium topic decks
- Session pass / couple plan payments
- Better question suggestions and templates
- Answer review UX improvements
- Privacy upgrades (auto-delete, screenshot warning)
- Post-Match “next conversation” checklist
- Post-No-Match support resources (non-exploitative)
- Analytics funnel instrumentation
- Basic moderation for abusive questions

#### Success metrics
- Free-to-paid conversion ≥ 3–8% of completed rooms (early benchmark)
- Deck attach rate
- Second-room reuse rate ≥ 15–25%
- Support tickets related to privacy remain low

Exit criteria:
- Repeatable paid conversion
- Clear top decks by demand

---

### Stage 3 — Trust Brand + Distribution (3–6 months)
**Goal:** Become the known ritual before engagement in a defined segment.

#### Build
- Counselor-reviewed certified decks
- Regional language support (start with one additional language)
- Conditional Yes / caveats (if validation supports it)
- Room history with dual consent
- Partner reminders / gentle nudges
- Referral program
- Lightweight admin tools

#### Growth
- Creator partnerships
- Premarital workshop pilots
- First matrimony / counselor B2B pilot

#### Success metrics
- Organic invite growth
- Brand recall in niche (“the big talk app”)
- Partnership pipeline active

Exit criteria:
- Stable weekly completed rooms growth
- At least one scalable acquisition channel beyond founder posting

---

### Stage 4 — Platform Expansion (6–12 months)
**Goal:** Expand from one-time game to relationship decision platform — without losing core identity.

#### Possible expansions (only after Stage 3 strength)
1. Lifecycle packs: engagement readiness, first-year marriage check-ins
2. Facilitator mode for counselors
3. White-label for matrimony platforms
4. Deeper finance / family planning integrations (affiliate, careful)
5. Multi-round “compatibility journal” for couples who matched

#### Guardrails
- Do not become a generic dating swipe app
- Do not dilute double-blind dignity mechanic
- Expand only into adjacent high-trust use cases

## 12. Technical Direction (Plan-Level)

### 12.1 Recommended architecture (MVP)
- Mobile-first app (React Native / Flutter) **or** high-quality PWA first for faster invite testing
- Backend with realtime room state (WebSockets / Firebase-style sync)
- Secure storage for questions/answers
- Server-enforced reveal rules (never trust client-only locks)

### 12.2 Critical technical requirements
- Atomic “both ready → reveal” transactions
- Verdict sealed until both submitted
- Auth security + rate limits on invites
- Data deletion workflows
- Audit logs for abuse investigation (access-controlled)

### 12.3 Build priority order
1. Room sync reliability  
2. Reveal integrity  
3. Invite conversion UX  
4. Content decks  
5. Payments  
6. Polish / animations

## 14. Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Why it matters | Mitigation |
|------|----------------|------------|
| Partner B does not join | Product dies at invite | Frictionless WhatsApp invite, preview of value, reminders |
| One-time usage only | Weak LTV | Lifecycle decks, couple plans, post-match follow-ups |
| Privacy incident | Brand death | Strong defaults, deletion, minimal data, security review |
| Emotional harm after No Match | Trust + ethics risk | Careful copy, resources, no blame UX |
| Dishonest answers | Perceived uselessness | Better prompts, optional scales, set expectations honestly |
| Copycats | Feature parity | Own brand ritual + certified content + partnerships |
| Positioning confusion | Wrong users acquire | Messaging: serious couples only, not casual dating |

## 19. Final Recommendation

Build this as a **serious, private clarity ritual**, not a casual dating toy.

**MVP focus:** perfect the 4-step loop and partner invite.  
**Better-version focus:** expert decks, privacy trust, post-verdict usefulness, and cultural depth.  
**Money focus:** pay for completed clarity (sessions/decks), then partnerships.  
**Growth focus:** WhatsApp couple invites + content that normalizes hard talks.

If the product makes saying the uncomfortable thing feel safer than silence, it can earn a durable place in the pre-marriage market.

## Appendix A — Glossary

- **Lobby:** Private room where both partners connect  
- **Simultaneous exchange:** Reveal only when both are ready  
- **Double-blind verdict:** Yes/No choices hidden until both vote; No identity never revealed  
- **Deck:** Curated set of topic questions  
- **Clarity session:** Full completed loop through verdict
